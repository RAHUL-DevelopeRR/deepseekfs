# Hyperframes composition brief

Build a 1080×1920, 15.5-second Brag cut for NeuCockpit from the project's existing local search and AI-mode positioning. Make the first frame legible as a hook. Use seven fast scenes with decisive cuts; three short white flashes and cyan edge wipes add energy without prolonged strobing. Hold each claim long enough to read. Keep UI examples visibly illustrative. Use white typography, minimal near-black canvas, and limited cyan/violet accents. End on NeuCockpit and zero-x.live.
